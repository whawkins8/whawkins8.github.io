# My Homepage!

Hello! I'm William, and welcome to my homepage! 

At present, my goal with this page is to give myself a place to publish things
that I write. My goal in writing is twofold and both of these goals are going to
be very boring for you: First, I find myself reading more and more, whether
books, articles, blog posts, whatever. I find that a lot of the time spent
reading is perhaps time wasted because I cannot remember what I have read, or
why a particular passage was so impactful on me at the time. So I'd like to log
here the books I have read and hopefully write a short review for them as well.
The other purpose is that my writing is not very good. I think the only way to
improve is by writing more. This is a vehicle to do that and a place to put
documents so they will not be lost. 

Like I said, these two reasons are very boring and not likely to inspire you to
read what I have here! Inspiring you to continue is not my goal, turn back now,
and do something better with your time than reading my site!
